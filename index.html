About BrightPath

What We Do

BrightPath Community Outreach Centre has been serving disadvantaged communities since 2018. We run three core programme streams:

  • Education — Tutoring, digital literacy, skills training
  • Wellness — Mental health workshops, counselling referrals
  • Community — Food drives, leadership camps, charity events

All programmes are free for qualifying community members.
