Volunteer Registration

Join our volunteer team and make a difference in the community. Whether you have a few hours a month or a few days a week, we have a role for you.

Available Volunteer Roles

Help learners from Grade 4–12 with their schoolwork. Sessions run Monday–Thursday, 14:00–17:00 at the centre. A teaching qualification is helpful but not required.

Share your tech skills by training community members in basic computing, Microsoft Office, internet use and social media literacy. Flexible scheduling available.

Facilitate monthly wellness workshops. Ideal for social workers, psychologists, nurses or wellness coaches. Remote facilitation also available via Zoom.

Help plan and run community events like food drives, career expos and leadership camps. Great for people with logistics, marketing or event management experience.
