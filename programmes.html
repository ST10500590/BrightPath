Our Programmes

All BrightPath programmes are free of charge to qualifying community members. Click on any programme to find out more.

📚

Youth Skills Training

We provide CV writing, interview preparation and digital literacy training to equip young people for the workforce.

  • CV and cover letter workshops
  • Interview simulation sessions
  • Microsoft Office and Google Workspace training
  • Internet safety and online job seeking
📝

Homework Support

After-school tutoring and mentoring programmes help students improve academically and build confidence.

  • Gr 4–12 tutoring in core subjects
  • One-on-one and group mentoring
  • Study skills and exam preparation
🤵

Wellness Support

Community wellness workshops and counselling referrals are offered to families in need of mental and emotional support.

  • Mental health awareness workshops
  • Stress management and coping strategies
  • Referrals to licensed counsellors
  • Support groups for youth and parents
🏠

Community Empowerment

We run initiatives that bring communities together and build local leadership capacity.

  • Youth leadership development
  • Community food drives
  • Parent and family support circles
  • Charity events and fundraisers
